## 2.0 - 2026-11-15

1. Added the tank, a main battle tank in the style of the M1 Abrams, at its real size (about 7.9 by 3.7 blocks) and in M81 woodland camouflage. It comes in its own military crate (see below): take it from the creative tab or make it in the advanced workshop.
2. The tank drives with the movement keys on tracks: it turns on the spot and at any speed. It needs no engine item and runs on fuel like the other vehicles. Its fuel filler is on the rear plate of the hull, above the exhaust grille, between the tail lights, at chest height so it can be seen and reached from the ground (the deck is at eye level): a steel housing with a filler neck and a round cap on a hinge. Hold the jerry can or the gas pump nozzle on it: the cap swings open on its hinge while the tank fills and shows the mouth of the neck, then closes.
3. The crew sits inside the tank with the hatches closed, out of sight (4 seats). The first player in is the driver: from his place at the front of the hull, at the height of his periscopes, he drives, aims and fires. The gunner (in his sight), the commander (in his cupola) and the loader (under his hatch) turn with the turret. The players and their hand are not drawn while inside, and the hotbar is hidden. In third person the camera turns around a point 2 blocks above the turret, so it looks over the turret and the gun at the target.
4. Aiming: the turret and the gun point at what is under the crosshair (a block or an entity), at a limited speed so the turret stays aimed while the hull turns. The gun elevation (-8 to +18 degrees) includes the drop of the projectile, so the shot lands on what is under the crosshair once the turret has caught up. A custom sight replaces the crosshair: the aiming mark at the centre, a marker showing where the shot would land with the gun as it points now (green when it is on the aiming mark), the distance to that point and the zoom.
5. Weapons, chosen with the keys 1 to 5 in a weapon bar with icons that replaces the hotbar (5 fires all the weapons at once: a click fires the gun, holding the attack key fires the machine guns and the light shells together): the 120 mm gun (5 seconds to reload after each shot, shown by a light veil over its icon in the weapon bar that goes down as the reload ends, like the cooldown of a vanilla item), the coaxial machine gun and the .50 cal heavy machine gun on the commander's cupola (they keep firing while the attack key is held, with tracers and impacts), and the light shells (fired 4 times per second while the attack key is held, out of the launch tubes on both sides of the turret in turn, and aimed at what is under the crosshair; each one explodes like a smaller shell). The bullets and shells fly the same way on every client as on the server (exact speed sent with them).
6. The 120 mm gun fires a shell (60 blocks per second, so its drop shows) with the sound of the shot, the recoil of the gun barrel, a kick back of the tank (the hull rocks, the side of the gun going up, and slides back a little; the driver's view kicks up), a muzzle blast (flash, tongue of fire, light smoke out of the barrel and a little dust), a hot tracer, a light smoke trail and its whistle in flight (the sound follows the shell). The shell explodes with its own explosion sound where it hits with a powerful explosion (power 7, TNT is 4): a big fire ball, a flash, a blast wave along the ground, sparks, pieces of the ground thrown in the air, a ring of dust the colour of the ground and a short column of dark smoke (2 to 3.5 seconds and about 3 blocks wide for a light shell, up to 5 seconds and 7 blocks for the 120 mm shell). The explosions hurt entities but do not break blocks.
7. Zoom: the mouse wheel zooms progressively from x1 to x10 (10 % per notch, smoothly), Alt + wheel by 0.01 and Ctrl + wheel by 0.1 for a precise zoom (x3.61 for example). The view turns slower when zoomed in, in proportion to the zoom.
8. The tracks are animated and the road wheels, idlers and drive sprockets turn with the distance travelled; when the tank turns, the two tracks move in opposite directions.
9. The tank is modelled as polygon meshes (Forge OBJ models), made by tools/concepts/tank/tank.js (not shipped): every part is a closed solid, the plates meet along shared edges without overlapping or leaving gaps, the slopes (glacis, turret cheeks, leaning turret sides) are real sloped faces, the outer edges (turret roof, bustle corners, side skirts, deck, gun shield, sight) are bevelled and the camouflage is laid like paint, at the same scale on every face: it runs on across the plates, goes on over the bevels and the edges of each plate without a break (the sloped faces and bevels are unfolded from the face next to them, so they are not stretched either), and wraps around the round parts (gun barrel, cupola, wheels) instead of forming stripes along them. The road wheels, idlers, drive sprockets and the gun barrel are round. The camouflage comes from the public domain M81 U.S. woodland pattern (Wikimedia Commons); the firing sounds (120 mm gun, light shells, machine guns) were provided by the mod author.
10. The effects use the mod's own particles (own textures of fire, smoke, flash and spark, made by tools/concepts/tank/particles.js) drawn with a soft particle shader: no hard edges on the smoke, and the fire glows. The thick parts of the smoke hide the clouds and the rain behind them, which no longer show through it. If the shader cannot be loaded, the effects still show with the vanilla particle shader.
11. Changed the version number to 2.0 and the network protocol version to 2: a 2.0 client cannot join a 1.x server and the other way round.
12. Ammunition: the tank fires only with ammunition (a player in creative mode needs none and uses none, like the fuel). Added the 120 mm tank shell (one shell per item, stacks of 10), the pack of light shells (4 light shells, stacks of 16) and the belt of cartridges (100 cartridges for both machine guns, stacks of 8). They have no recipe yet: take them from the creative tab. From inside the tank, the inventory key opens the tank's store ("Ammunition and Armour"), like the inventory of a donkey, in a screen of its own in the look of the tank (a panel painted in the tank's camouflage, with dark steel modules): one module for each ammunition with the icon of its weapon on a raised plate, 4 slots that only take that ammunition (an outline of it shows in the empty ones), its name, the rounds the tank has and a gauge of how full it is, leaning like the bars of the HUD (40 shells, 256 light shells and 3200 cartridges when full); the armour module (see below); and the player's inventory below. The "Load all" button moves all the ammunition and armour plates of the player's inventory into the tank in one click (done by the server through the slots, so nothing can be doubled; greyed out when there is nothing to load). The store is saved with the tank and its contents drop when the tank is destroyed. The weapon bar shows the rounds left above each weapon (red when there are none), and a weapon without ammunition only clicks.
13. The state of the tank shows in the bottom left corner for the whole crew: health, armour, fuel and speed (in blocks per second), each with its icon, a bar and its value, straight over the view (no panel behind them); it replaces the speed and fuel text of the other vehicles. The bars lean to the right at both ends, are outlined and made of 16 segments leaning like them: the filled ones in the colour of the bar, the others faint. The armour bar is cut in 5 small bars, one per armour plate (front, left, top, right, rear), each filled to what its plate has left; a missing plate leaves its part empty. In the tank the experience bar is hidden, and the player's hearts, armour and food move up above the weapon bar and its counts.
14. Fixed the rear wheel of the moped not showing (reported for 1.3): its size was missing from the moped's properties, so it was drawn at size 0 (already the case in the original 0.45.2). It now gets the size of the front wheel, as in the development version of the mod.
15. Fixed the first person view of the sports plane (reported for 1.3): when the plane climbed or dived, its body tilted but the pilot's eyes stayed level and in place, so the nose of the plane came in front of the view. The eyes now move with the cockpit when the plane pitches or banks, and the view goes up and down with the nose like it already turned with the plane (client option rotateCameraWithVehicle). This applies to the bath too, which flies like the plane.
16. Added the advanced workshop, made on a crafting table from the mod's workstation (in the middle), a nether star (above), 2 circuit boards (on its sides), a servo motor (below) and 4 iron blocks (in the corners), where the tank and its ammunition are made (and later the other special vehicles): a machining centre of 3 by 2 blocks, 2.5 high (it stands on a base half a block high, so its window is at eye level), placed to the right of and behind the block aimed at, facing the player. Its outline and collisions follow the shape of the machine. Its casing is made of titanium plates fitted to each face (as many plates of the same size as fit, a joint line all round each and a screw in each corner), its edges and its base dark; its cables go in and out through cable glands. Its screen lists its recipes; the chosen one shows a turning preview of the vehicle (or the item), its energy and time, and its materials with how many are in the machine. With the materials in its 12 input slots, the start button takes them and the work goes through the phases of the recipe (for the tank: armour plates, hull and tracks, engine, turret and gun, tests and crating), using energy (FE) every tick; it waits when the energy runs out, and cancelling gives the materials back. The result goes to the output slot (the tank comes in its crate). Through the door of the machine, the vehicle is built as a miniature on the table: at the start it is a transparent blue hologram, and the real vehicle fills it from the bottom up, point by point like a 3D printer: each layer is cut into small points, made one after the other row by row, while the assembly head sweeps very fast over the point being made with its red laser always on, which glows white hot and throws sparks where it hits. The ammunition is made the same way in 3D: the shells standing in a row, the light shells in packs of four, the belt in its ammunition can. Three small lights above the door show its state: green while it works, amber blinking without energy, red blinking when the output is full. The console screen lights up when the machine has energy and shows the recipe being made, its phase (a long phase name scrolls), the percentage done, the bars of the phase and of the whole work, and the energy (or why the machine waits); the bars do not flicker (their filled part and their track only meet, they no longer cover each other). The machine makes sounds when it starts, while it works (a hum, the head moving, the laser welding), when it finishes and when it is cancelled. It costs little frame time: the still machine is a block model baked with the chunk, and the miniature is kept on the graphics card (only the layer being made is sent each frame). The machine takes energy from the cables of other mods and items from hoppers and pipes on any of its faces. The recipes are data files (data/vehicle_restored/recipes/advanced_workshop) and use: the tank (10 iron blocks, 32 chains for the tracks, a diamond large engine, 2 servo motors, 3 circuit boards and a spyglass for the sight; 3 minutes; it comes without armour plates), 4 tank shells, 2 packs of light shells, a belt of cartridges. Its item fits in an inventory slot and shows the front of the machine (its door and console), like the front of a vanilla furnace.
17. Added the creative battery (creative tab only): endless energy given to every block touching it that takes energy, to try the advanced workshop without an energy mod.
18. Added the military crate, in which the tank comes (from the creative tab and from the advanced workshop) instead of the vehicle crate: a wooden transport crate of 2 by 2 blocks, 1.5 high, on three skids, painted olive drab (its frame a little darker than its planks), raw wood inside, with steel corner caps, two handles on each side and latches holding its lid. It carries stencilled markings: TOP SECRET on the lid and as a red stamp on its front, a paper notice with a bar code, the handling marks (this way up, keep dry, fragile, sling here, centre of gravity), no smoking and danger signs, the contents and the lot number. It is placed to the right of and behind the block aimed at, facing the player; breaking any of its blocks gives it back with the tank inside. Like the vehicle crate, it opens with a wrench on its lid (at once when placed in creative), if the blocks around it are free: the lid is thrown off over one side (never where the tank comes out), turns over once in the air and lands flat on the ground 3 to 4 blocks away with a thud, then breaks apart in splinters, the four walls fall outwards one after the other and lie on the ground, and the tank grows out of it. Its item fits in an inventory slot, its front and lid the right way round. Closed, it is a block model baked with the chunk (it costs no frame time); its model and its texture (every face drawn at its exact size, so the planks, joints, edges and markings fall exactly on the shape) are made by tools/concepts/crate/crate.js (not shipped).
19. The tank has 500 health (the other vehicles 100): a 120 mm shell takes up to 99, a light shell up to 50, a machine gun bullet 5 or 10, so it takes 5 or 6 shells to destroy it. Tanks saved before keep their damage in proportion.
20. Fixed vehicles destroyed at once by a shell or a bullet fired by a player in creative mode (from a tank, for example): only a player in creative mode hitting a vehicle directly breaks it at once, what they fire takes health like any other damage.
21. Armour of the tank: 5 armour plates, one on each face (front, left, top, right, rear), put on from the tank's screen, where the tank is drawn from above (in its camouflage) with a socket for each face (hovering an empty one names its face). A tank made in the workshop has none. A plate has 100 armour points and takes the hits on its face (from where the explosion, the projectile or the attacker comes; from above when it falls on the roof); what it cannot take goes to the health, and a hit with no direction is shared by the plates. The armour does not come back: a plate is worn for good (its durability bar shows it), breaks with the sound of a breaking shield when worn through and can then be replaced by a new one; it cannot be repaired (neither in the crafting grid nor at the anvil). Damage that goes through armour in vanilla (fall, burning, drowning...) goes straight to the health. The tank's health is not repaired either (no hammer repair on the jack). The plates drop when the tank is destroyed. The screen shows the armour left in percent of 5 new plates.
22. Added the armour plate (crafting table: 6 iron ingots in two rows with a row of 3 bricks between them, the steel, ceramic, steel of composite armour), and two components for the advanced vehicles, made on the crafting table and used by the advanced workshop: the circuit board (copper, redstone, nether quartz and a gold ingot: 2 boards) and the servo motor (iron, copper, a piston and redstone).
23. JEI support (optional): with Just Enough Items installed, the recipes of the advanced workshop, the vehicle workstation, the fluid extractor and the fluid mixer show in JEI, each machine as their catalyst; the vehicles show in their crate.

## 1.3 - 2026-11-11

1. Fixed the fuel of the gas pump, the fuel drums, the fluid extractor and the fluid mixer that was sometimes lost when the world was reloaded. A change of fluid did not mark the block as changed, so it was only saved when something else changed in the chunk.
2. Fixed the fluid extractor and the fluid mixer not starting with ingredients that carry extra data, for example items taken from the creative tab or from another mod. The ingredient is now recognised by its item, like in vanilla recipes.
3. Fixed vehicles veering left or right with a gamepad whose stick does not come back exactly to the centre. Small sideways inputs no longer steer the vehicle.
4. The camera now follows the seat when a flying helicopter tilts: in first person it no longer ends up behind the seats at full speed, and the third person camera turns around the tilted seat.
5. The fluid in the gas pump now stays inside the glass of its model, instead of sticking out at the front, at the back and at the top, and its sides show through the glass.
6. The 3D preview of the vehicle crates measures each vehicle, centres it on its turning axis and scales it to fit the preview, so the large vehicles such as the sports plane and the golf cart no longer overflow it and the small ones are no longer tiny.

## 1.2 - 2026-11-09

1. Fixed vehicles that could not be refueled, neither with a jerry can nor with the gas pump. Holding the right mouse button on the fuel port did nothing: the port checked the "use" key, which never registers as held because the mod takes over that click. It now checks the right mouse button itself, as the original mod did.

## 1.1 - 2026-11-09

1. Fixed a crash at startup with Immersive Vehicles and its content packs ("Unable to fit" while stitching textures). The block texture atlas took the "model", "vehicle" and "vehicles" texture folders of every mod, and the large vehicle textures of these packs did not fit in it. It now only takes the textures of Vehicle Restored.

## 1.0

1. Ported the 0.45.2 version of the Vehicle Mod (Minecraft 1.16.5) to Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.
2. Replaced the Obfuscate library with code inside the mod: the data synced on players (trailer being hitched, gas pump nozzle in hand) and the events used to pose the player model. The mod no longer needs any other mod.
3. Removed the support for the Controllable gamepad mod.
4. Added the sports car, the quad bike and the compact helicopter from the unreleased development version of the mod, adapted to the vehicle system of the released version, with their workstation recipes. Their openable doors, hood and trunk are not included.
5. Added a model loader that reads the model format of MrCrayfish's Framework library, used by the models of these three vehicles, so the mod does not need Framework.
6. The furniture vehicles (sofa, bath, sofacopter) now work with Furniture Restored instead of MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod, and use its sofa, bath and ceiling fan.
7. The bath now renders the bath of Furniture Restored. In 0.45.2 it showed the ATV body as a placeholder.
8. The sofacopter rotor now renders the blades of the ceiling fan of Furniture Restored, as in the 1.12.2 version.
9. Added the workstation recipes of the bath and the sofacopter, which were missing in 0.45.2, using the ingredients of the 1.12.2 version. The sofa vehicle is crafted with a red sofa.
10. The surfaces under the wheels are now recognised with block tags, because 1.20 removed block materials. The categories are the same as in 0.45.2.
11. The key bindings now show in the controls menu.
12. The speedometer is drawn as a HUD overlay.
13. The vehicles from the furniture mod are ignored instead of causing an error when a world that contains them is opened without Furniture Restored.
14. Fixed the vehicle crate forgetting who opened it after the world is reloaded.
15. Fixed a crash when a client joins a dedicated server, caused by the workstation ingredient not being registered.
16. Updated the recipes, loot tables, advancements, models and block tags to the 1.20.1 formats, and added the missing tags for mining with a pickaxe or an axe.
17. Removed the data generators of the original project; the resources they generated are kept as files.
18. Added a French translation of the mod, and the missing English texts.
19. Renamed the mod to Vehicle Restored, changed the mod ID from vehicle to vehicle_restored, and renamed the jar to VehicleRestored.
20. Changed the version number to 1.0.
21. Removed the update link, the logo, the issue tracker link and the website link of the original mod, and rewrote the mod information shown in the mods list.
22. Removed the README, the Forge credits and the Forge license of the original project.
23. The traffic cone, the workstation, the gas pump, its nozzle and the jerry cans use their models from the 1.12.2 version again, with the vanilla textures of 1.20.1. The gas pump screen shows the fill level and the fuel name again, as in 1.12.2.
24. The hitboxes of the traffic cone and the workstation follow their models.
25. Holding shift over a vehicle crate shows a turning 3D preview of the vehicle inside, with its color, engine and wheels.
26. The bath and the sofacopter have a vehicle crate in the creative tab. The sofa vehicle uses the red sofa, like the sofacopter, and is crafted with it.
27. The third person camera is further back for large vehicles (compact helicopter, sports plane, mini bus, off-roader, golf cart, tractor, sports car), with the distances of the development version of the mod.
28. Fixed the thin slits at the corners of the vehicle crate.
29. Fixed the missing particles when breaking a fuel drum.
30. Fixed a crash when a smart car, bumper car, dune buggy or go-kart was created with recent versions of Forge.
31. The mod writes its config files with their default values, so Forge no longer logs warnings when it creates them.
32. Fixed the bath not moving when driven: it has no engine, and the plane controls only accelerated with an engine installed.
33. Fixed endless chests with the moped: attaching a chest now takes it from the player's hand (except in creative mode).
34. A vehicle can only be picked up by its owner or by an operator, and only within reach. Trailers have no owner and can still be picked up by anyone.
35. Only the owner or a passenger of a vehicle can take its key out, and only within reach. A blank key no longer causes an error.
36. A player can only hitch a trailer to themselves, and only within reach.
37. The server now checks the network messages sent by the clients: each message can only travel in its own direction, picking up a vehicle, using its key, hitching a trailer and opening or attaching a chest need the player to be within reach, and invalid steering, power and helicopter values are rejected.
38. The workstation menu closes when the player is more than 8 blocks away or the workstation is gone, and the dye, engine and wheels inside it drop when it is broken.
39. The workstation slots only accept one item of the right type, also from hoppers and pipes, and crafting a vehicle uses one dye, one engine and one wheel instead of the whole stacks. Vehicles without an engine type no longer ask for an engine.
40. Fixed an error that could stop crafting at the workstation halfway through and lose the materials already taken, and fixed shift-clicking items in the workstation menu.
41. The storage menus of the trailers and the moped close when the player is more than 8 blocks away.
42. The lawn mower, the seeder and the fertilizer respect the spawn protection and the claim mods: they only change blocks where their driver is allowed to, and follow the mobGriefing rule when nobody drives them.
43. The seeder only plants on the server side, so it no longer leaves ghost crops on the client, and only uses a seed when the crop stays. It no longer picks saplings from a storage trailer.
